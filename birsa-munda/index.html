What the sources say

Facts & sources

  • Birsa Munda was born on 15 November 1875 in Ulihatu, into a poor Munda family that kept moving in search of work.
  • He studied at Salga under Jaipal Nag, then joined the German Mission School and was renamed Birsa David. Later he left school, and in 1890 his family returned to the traditional tribal religion.
  • Thikadars and the forest laws were taking away the land and rights of the Mundas, and Birsa saw this closely.
  • In 1895 he had a vision of a supreme God and became a healer and preacher. People began calling him Dharti Aaba.
  • He declared that Queen Victoria's rule was over and the Munda Raj had begun, and he told tenants to pay no rent. He was arrested on 24 August 1895 and sentenced to two years in jail.
  • After his release on 28 January 1898 he went underground. Around Christmas 1899 about 7000 people gathered and the Ulgulan began.
  • In January 1900 there were attacks, a reward of Rs 500 was set on him, and police fired at Sail Rakab Hill. The Dumbari Hill fight was lost, and Birsa was arrested on 3 February 1900 at Jamkopai forest.
  • Birsa died in jail on 9 June 1900. In 1908 the Chotanagpur Tenancy Act stopped the transfer of tribal land to non-tribals, and today 15 November is Janjatiya Gaurav Divas.

Source: Birsa Munda (Wikipedia) and the texts it cites.

Quiz

How much do you remember?

5 questions. Every answer is in this story.

1. बिरसा मुंडा का जन्म कहाँ हुआ था?

उनका जन्म 15 नवंबर 1875 को उलिहातू में एक गरीब मुंडा परिवार में हुआ।

2. जर्मन मिशन स्कूल में उनका नाम क्या रखा गया?

मिशन स्कूल में दाख़िले के बाद उनका नाम बिरसा डेविड रखा गया।

3. 1895 में बिरसा को क्या सज़ा मिली?

24 अगस्त 1895 को गिरफ़्तारी हुई और दो साल की कैद मिली।

4. बिरसा को किस जगह के जंगल से गिरफ़्तार किया गया?

3 फ़रवरी 1900 को जामकोपाई के जंगल में वे गिरफ़्तार हुए।

5. बिरसा की मौत के बाद 1908 में कौन सा कानून बना?

इस कानून ने आदिवासी ज़मीन के गैर-आदिवासियों को हस्तांतरण पर रोक लगाई।
