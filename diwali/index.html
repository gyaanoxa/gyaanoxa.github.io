What the sources say

Facts & sources

  • Diwali comes from Dipavali, 'a row of lights'. The five-day festival begins two days before the new moon (amavasya) with Dhanteras and ends two days after it. It is likely a fusion of ancient harvest festivals.
  • Dhanteras legend: King Hima's 16-year-old son was fated to die of a snakebite on the fourth day of his marriage. His bride piled ornaments and gold and silver coins at the door, lit lamps and told stories all night. Yama came as a serpent, was dazzled, sat on the heap all night and left silently in the morning.
  • Dhanvantari, the god of Ayurveda, is said to have emerged from the Samudra Manthana with a pot of amrita. India observes Dhanteras as National Ayurveda Day, first held on 28 October 2016.
  • Naraka Chaturdashi (Chhoti Diwali): Narakasura, the evil king of Pragjyotishapura near present-day Assam, held 16,000 princesses captive. One source says Krishna killed him, another says Krishna and Satyabhama did. In Bengal, 14 diyas are lit for the 14 forefathers on Bhoot Chaturdashi.
  • The third day is the main Diwali and the darkest night. It marks Rama, Sita, Lakshmana and Hanuman reaching Ayodhya after 14 years of exile. It is also Lakshmi Puja, and Lakshmi is believed to visit the cleanest house first.
  • Jains mark Mahavira's nirvana. Their tradition says lamps were first lit in 527 BCE, when 18 kings proclaimed them in his memory. Sikhs celebrate Bandi Chhor Divas, the release of Guru Hargobind from Jahangir's prison at Gwalior Fort.
  • Govardhan Puja / Annakut: Krishna lifted Govardhan Hill on his little finger, and after seven days of storms Indra accepted defeat. The Guinness record for the largest Annakut, over 3500 dishes, was set on October 27, 2019 at BAPS Atladra Mandir in Gujarat.
  • Bhai Dooj (Yamadvitiya) is linked to a legendary meeting between Yama and his sister Yamuna. Another belief says Krishna visited his sister Subhadra after killing Narakasura. In 1905, Tagore drew on this ritual for rakhi ceremonies between Hindus and Muslims, held to protest the Partition of Bengal.

Source: Diwali (Wikipedia) and the texts it cites. Legends are told as legends.

Quiz

कितना याद रहा?

5 सवाल। सारे जवाब इसी कहानी में हैं।

1. 'दीपावली' शब्द का अर्थ क्या है?

दीपावली = दीप + आवली, यानी दीपों की पंक्ति।

2. धनतेरस को भारत किस रूप में भी मनाता है?

पहला राष्ट्रीय आयुर्वेद दिवस 28 अक्टूबर 2016 को मनाया गया।

3. नरकासुर ने कितनी राजकुमारियों को कैद रखा था?

कथा के अनुसार नरकासुर ने 16,000 राजकुमारियों को कैद किया था।

4. सिख दिवाली के दिन कौन-सा पर्व मनाते हैं?

ग्वालियर किले से गुरु हरगोबिंद जी की रिहाई की याद में बंदी छोड़ दिवस।

5. कथा के अनुसार कृष्ण ने गोवर्धन पर्वत कितने दिन उठाए रखा?

सात दिन की बारिश के बाद इंद्र ने हार मानी।
