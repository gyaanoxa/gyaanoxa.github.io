Data Deletion Instructions

Last updated: 5 November 2026

Gyaanoxa Studio is a private tool that reads only our own YouTube and Instagram accounts. It does not store personal data of other users. If you believe we hold any data about you, or you want it removed, you can ask us to delete it.

How to request deletion

  1. Send an email to gyaanoxa@gmail.com with the subject "Data deletion request".
  2. Tell us your Instagram username, YouTube channel or email address, so we can find the data.
  3. We delete the data within 30 days and reply to confirm.

Removing the app's access

You can also remove an app's access to your Instagram account yourself: in Instagram, go to Settings → Website permissions → Apps and websites, find the app and tap Remove. For Google or YouTube, go to myaccount.google.com/permissions and remove access.
