Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 November 2026

This policy explains what data Gyaanoxa ("we") handles on this website, on our social media channels and in Gyaanoxa Studio, the private tool we use to make and manage our own videos. Contact: gyaanoxa@gmail.com.

1. This website

This is a static website hosted on GitHub Pages. We do not use analytics, ads, sign-ups or forms, and we set no cookies unless you choose a language (see below). GitHub, as the host, may log technical data such as your IP address for security; see the GitHub Privacy Statement. Video thumbnails are loaded from YouTube's image servers, and fonts from Google Fonts.

If you choose a language from the 🌐 language menu, the page is translated by Google Translate: only then is Google's translation script loaded, and Google may set cookies and process the page text and your IP address under the Google Privacy Policy. Your language choice is remembered in a small cookie named googtrans. Pick "Original" to switch it off.

2. Gyaanoxa Studio and connected accounts

Gyaanoxa Studio connects only to our own YouTube channel and Instagram account, with our permission, through the official Google (YouTube Data and Analytics) and Meta (Instagram) APIs. It is used only by the channel owner.

  • What it reads: our own account's basic profile (name, username, ID), the list of our own posts and videos, and their public counts and insights (views, reach, likes, comments, shares, saves, watch time, follower counts).
  • What it writes: on YouTube only — titles, descriptions, tags, thumbnails, captions, playlists and schedule times of our own videos. It does not post, comment or message on Instagram.
  • Where data is stored: only on the owner's own computer. Access tokens are stored locally and are never shared.
  • Other people's data: we do not collect personal data of viewers or followers. Insights are aggregated numbers provided by the platforms.
  • Sharing and selling: we never sell, rent or share data with anyone, and we do not use it for advertising.

3. If you contact us

If you email us, we use your email address and message only to reply to you, and delete them on request.

4. Comments on our videos

Comments you post on YouTube or Instagram are handled by those platforms under their own privacy policies. We may reply to them or pin them.

5. Your rights and deletion

You can ask us what data we hold about you, or ask us to delete it, at any time. See Data Deletion Instructions. We answer within 30 days.

6. Children

This website and our tools are not directed at children under 13 and do not knowingly collect their data.

7. Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
